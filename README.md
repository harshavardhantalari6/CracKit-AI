CrackIt AI: Complete Startup Blueprint & Architectural Overview1. Executive Summary & VisionCrackIt AI is an AI-powered, adaptive Prep-to-Placement platform built to streamline career preparation for both Government Competitive Examinations (UPSC CSE, SSC CGL, Banking, Railways) and Private IT Sector Technical Drives (TCS NQT, Infosys, Amazon, Product & Service companies).Unlike traditional test-prep platforms that rely on static, repetitive question banks and generic answer keys, CrackIt AI leverages generative intelligence to provide a dynamic, hyper-personalized training ecosystem.
